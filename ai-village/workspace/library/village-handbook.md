# Skillsbury village handbook

The Town Hall holds the task inbox. Requests without a type are triaged by the Mayor into research, email or digest work.

The Library is where research happens. Researchers only cite documents that exist in the library or research folders.

The Post Office drafts emails into the outbox folder. Drafts are never sent automatically; a human reviews and sends every one.

The Noticeboard is the record of completed work. Every finished task gets one line, and the Scribe writes a daily digest at the end of the work day.
